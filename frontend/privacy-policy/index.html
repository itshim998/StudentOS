This policy explains what StudentOS collects, why it is needed, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. About StudentOS

StudentOS is a study workspace provided by SentIQ AI Labs. It helps students organise courses, assignments, materials, tests, revision, and day-to-day study work.

This policy applies when you visit the StudentOS website, create an account, upload academic material, connect Google Classroom, or use StudentOS study features.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create or use an account, StudentOS may hold your name, email address, sign-in details, account status, plan selection, and the choices you make during setup.

Academic information

You may provide details such as your school or college, course or stream, class or semester, subjects, timetable, exam pattern, goals, deadlines, weak topics, and preferred study routine.

Materials and work you add

StudentOS may hold the files, notes, syllabi, timetables, assignment details, questions, answers, test responses, correction sheets, and other study material that you choose to add.

Your activity in StudentOS

StudentOS records the actions needed to maintain your workspace, such as completed tasks, study progress, test results, revision history, saved conversations, account preferences, and requests to export or delete information.

Basic service information

StudentOS may record limited information needed to keep the service reliable and secure, such as the time of a request, whether it succeeded, and information used to prevent misuse. We do not use StudentOS to sell advertising profiles.

3. Google Classroom information

Connecting Google Classroom is optional. When you choose to connect it, StudentOS may access:

  • your Google account name and email address;
  • your active Google Classroom courses;
  • published coursework, questions, descriptions, due dates, points, and related dates;
  • published classwork materials and the titles or links supplied with them; and
  • the status of your own Classroom submissions where Google permits it.

StudentOS uses this information to show your coursework, identify upcoming or pending work, let you choose what to include in your academic context, and organise study plans around your real classes.

StudentOS uses Google Classroom in read-only mode.

It does not submit or turn in work, edit coursework, grade work, post comments, email teachers, or read the contents of files stored in Google Drive through the Classroom connection. Where an attachment is shown, StudentOS uses the title, type, or link made available by Google Classroom.

You can disconnect Google Classroom from StudentOS. Disconnecting stops future access. Coursework already added to your StudentOS workspace may remain there until you remove it or ask us to delete your information.

StudentOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How we use information

We use your information only for purposes connected with providing and protecting StudentOS. This includes:

  • creating and maintaining your account;
  • building and updating your academic workspace;
  • organising courses, materials, assignments, deadlines, tests, and revision;
  • answering your questions and preparing study explanations, plans, notes, quizzes, and feedback;
  • remembering the academic context you have chosen to keep;
  • keeping Google Classroom connections working when you have enabled them;
  • handling support, account export, and deletion requests;
  • protecting accounts and preventing misuse; and
  • maintaining the reliability of the service.

We do not sell your personal information or Google Classroom information. We do not use Google Classroom information for advertising.

5. When information is shared

StudentOS relies on service providers that help us run the website, keep accounts and files, deliver requested study features, and protect the service. They receive only the information needed to perform their role for StudentOS.

When you ask StudentOS to work with your academic material, the relevant parts of that material may be sent to a service that processes the request and returns the result. This may include material you selected from Google Classroom when it is necessary to provide the feature you requested.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect users, StudentOS, and others from fraud, abuse, or harm.

StudentOS does not make your academic workspace public and does not share it with teachers, parents, mentors, or institutions unless you take an action that clearly enables such sharing. No such sharing is enabled by default.

6. Storage and retention

Your account and academic information are kept while your StudentOS account is active and for as long as they are needed to provide the features you use.

You can remove individual materials from your academic context. StudentOS may keep limited records needed for account security, support, consent history, legal compliance, and the safe handling of export or deletion requests.

Short-lived working copies, previews, and failed processing records may be removed automatically. StudentOS does not intentionally remove academic material that you selected or uploaded merely to make room for other material.

When Google Classroom is connected, the access details that allow StudentOS to refresh your Classroom information are kept away from the public website and protected from other users. Disconnecting Classroom prevents further access.

7. Your choices

You can control much of the information in StudentOS from your account and academic context settings. Depending on the feature available to your account, you may:

  • review or update your profile and academic setup;
  • add, remove, or replace academic material;
  • connect or disconnect Google Classroom;
  • request a copy of your StudentOS information;
  • change available consent preferences; and
  • request deletion of your account and workspace.

Account export and deletion requests are checked securely before they are completed. Disconnecting a service does not automatically erase information that was already imported into your StudentOS workspace.

8. Keeping information safe

We use access controls, private account storage, protected service credentials, and other safeguards intended to prevent unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

No online service can promise complete security. You should use a strong, unique password, keep your sign-in details private, and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

9. Younger users

StudentOS is intended for students. If you are under 18, you should use StudentOS only with the permission and supervision required by your parent, guardian, school, or local law.

Do not add another person's private information to StudentOS unless you have the right to do so.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when StudentOS changes or when legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version. Where an important change affects how your information is used, StudentOS may ask you to review the updated policy.
